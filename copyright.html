Copyright and takedown requests

Last updated: 28 October 2026

ClipsJudge is a clipping tool. Our users must have the right to clip what they submit. If you believe clips made or stored with ClipsJudge infringe your copyright, tell us and we'll act quickly.

How to send a takedown request

Email clipjudge.app@gmail.com with the subject "Copyright takedown" and include:

  1. Your name, contact details, and the rights holder you represent (if not yourself).
  2. The original work (for example the channel, stream or video link).
  3. Where the infringing material is: the ClipsJudge clip page link, or other details that help us find it.
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use isn't authorised by the rights holder, its agent or the law.
  5. A statement that the information in your request is accurate and that you're the rights holder or authorised to act for them.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name is enough).

What we do

  • We review complete requests and usually remove or disable the material within 2 business days.
  • We tell the user whose content was removed.
  • Accounts with repeated valid complaints are closed.

If your content was removed by mistake

Email us with the subject "Counter-notice", explaining why you have the right to use the content (for example your own stream, or the creator's permission), with your contact details and signature. We'll review it and, where appropriate, restore the content and let the complainant know.

Please note that clips users post on other platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and others) are outside ClipsJudge; report those directly to that platform.
