Billing questions

How do I pay?

Each plan is a 30-day pass paid in advance. In India you pay in rupees with UPI, cards or netbanking through Razorpay. Payments are processed securely by our payment partners; we never see or store your card details. Applicable taxes may be added at checkout depending on your country.

Does it renew automatically?

No. When your 30 days end, you can renew from your account. Renewing before your pass ends adds 30 days on top of the time you have left.

What happens when my hours run out?

Clipping stops until you add hours, upgrade, or renew. Clips you already have stay in your account for your plan's storage time.

Can I get a refund?

Because the free trial lets you test everything first, payments aren't refundable, except for billing mistakes or a fault on our side. See our refund and cancellation policy.

How is the service delivered?

ClipsJudge is an online software service. Access starts right after sign-up or payment; clips are delivered in your account for download. Nothing physical is shipped.
