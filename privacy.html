Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data ClipsJudge (India) collects, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. We collect only what we need to run the service.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, name if you give it, and login information.
  • Waitlist details: if you join the waitlist, your email, the streamers you clip, how many clips you post and where.
  • Payment details: handled by our payment partner. We receive only basic records such as your plan, payment status and country, never your full card number.
  • What you ask us to clip: stream and video links, your watch list, uploaded videos, campaign briefs you paste or upload, and the clips, transcripts, scores and titles we make from them.
  • Usage and technical data: hours used, settings, error logs, IP address, browser and device type, used to run, secure and improve the service.
  • Messages: emails and assistant chats you send us.

2. Why we use it

  • To provide the service: record and process the streams and videos you choose, make clips and deliver them to you.
  • To manage your account, plan limits and billing.
  • To send service emails (for example "clips ready", usage warnings, receipts) and, if you agree, occasional product news. You can unsubscribe anytime.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse (such as repeated free trials) and fix problems.
  • To improve clip quality, for example by learning which kinds of clips users download.

We don't sell your personal data, and we don't use your videos to train third-party AI models.

3. Who helps us (service providers)

We share data only with providers that help us run ClipsJudge, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Hosting: our servers (Hostinger) and website hosting (GitHub Pages).
  • AI processing: Anthropic (Claude), which receives short transcripts and small video frames of each moment to score and title clips, and the campaign briefs you add, to read them and match clips to them.
  • Speech to text: Groq, which receives audio from the streams and videos we clip, to write subtitles and find what was said.
  • Payments: our payment partner (such as Dodo Payments), which processes payments and may act as merchant of record.
  • Storage and email: cloud storage for clips (such as Cloudflare) and an email delivery service.
  • Phone alerts: the notification service you choose to receive alerts through.

Some providers are outside your country, including in the United States and India. We use providers that protect data with appropriate safeguards.

4. How long we keep it

  • Clips: for the period your plan includes (for example 30 or 60 days), then deleted automatically.
  • Uploaded videos: deleted a few days after they're processed.
  • Account data: while your account is open, and deleted within 30 days after you delete it, except records we must keep by law (such as payment and tax records).
  • Logs: kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU and UK GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you can ask to access, correct, download or delete your personal data, object to or limit some uses, and withdraw consent. Email clipjudge.app@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you logged in and the service working. We don't use advertising cookies.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), password protection, limited access and regular backups. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children

ClipsJudge isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We'll post updates here and, for important changes, email you.
