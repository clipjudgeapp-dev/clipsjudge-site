01

Four steps, once per campaign.

After that, clipping starts by itself when the campaign's streamers go live, within your plan's hours.

  1. 01

    Paste the brief

    Copy the campaign's brief from Whop or the program's page, or upload its PDF (Pro).

  2. 02

    Check what the AI read

    The moments they want, what to avoid, posting rules, length, hashtags and credit. Fix anything that's off.

  3. 03

    Pick the streamers

    They're clipped by themselves when they go live, within your plan's hours and live slots. Set a start date if the campaign begins later.

  4. 04

    Post the 🎯 clips

    Clips that fit the brief are marked 🎯 and you can sort by best fit, with the caption ready. You check each one, then post it yourself.

02

On every clip.

The normal clip score (is it a good moment?) plus a campaign score (is it what they asked for?).

Fits brief, out of 10

Each clip of the campaign's streamers gets a second score: how well it matches what the campaign asked for, with the AI's reason.

Caption ready to paste

The campaign's required hashtags and credit line go into the copied caption.

Rules where you can see them

Posting rules (logos, language, platforms) sit on the campaign card, and clips outside the length limits are flagged.

Quiet moments too

While the stream is live, the AI also looks for the brief's moments, not only the loud ones.

03

Joined late? Clip the recording.

Paste a past Twitch, Kick or YouTube stream and pick the campaign. Only want one moment from a 6-hour VOD? Describe it, and it uses half the hours of the part it checks.

04

What it doesn't do.

So you know before you pay.

  • It doesn't post or submit clips for you. AI can be wrong, so every clip waits for you to check it.
  • It doesn't decide whether a campaign accepts a clip or pays for it. The campaign does.
  • Clips are 30 seconds or shorter. If a campaign needs longer clips, ClipsJudge isn't the right fit for it yet.
  • You need the right to clip the stream, for example an official clipping program.

It saves you the watching. The posting decision stays yours.

Questions

Does it work with Whop Content Rewards?

Yes, with any campaign that gives you a written brief: Whop Content Rewards, a streamer's own clipping program, or a brand deal. You paste the brief into ClipsJudge; there's no account link to Whop. ClipsJudge isn't affiliated with Whop.

I joined the campaign late. Can I still get clips?

Yes. Paste the stream's recording (a Twitch, Kick or YouTube link) and choose the campaign. The campaign's moments are looked for in it. Set From and To to check only a part.

What does looking for one moment cost?

Describe the moment (for example "his reaction to the trailer") and it uses half the hours of that part of the video, because only the AI read looks for it.

Does a high Fits brief score mean the campaign will accept it?

No. It's the AI's opinion of how well the clip matches the brief, and it can be wrong. Watch the clip and read the campaign's rules before you post.

How many campaigns can I run?

1 at a time on the trial and Starter, 10 on Pro (with PDF briefs). See pricing.
