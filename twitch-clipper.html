02

What you get.

The same for every Twitch streamer you follow.

  • Starts by itself when the streamer goes live, no link to paste each time
  • Reads Twitch chat, voice, movement and what was said to find moments
  • An AI judge watches each clip; only 6+ clips reach you by default
  • Vertical 9:16 with subtitles, for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels
  • Caption that credits the creator, plus hashtags, copied in one tap
  • Past streams too: paste a link like twitch.tv/videos/…

Live clipping uses your plan's hours. If more of your streamers are live than your plan clips at once, the others wait for a free slot. AI scores and captions can be wrong, so check each clip before you post it.

Questions

How is it different from Twitch's own clips?

Twitch clips are made by hand, by viewers or the streamer. ClipsJudge finds the moments itself, makes vertical clips with subtitles, and scores each one, so you can decide which to post first.

Can I clip a Twitch VOD after the stream?

Yes. Paste the past broadcast's link (twitch.tv/videos/…) and it's clipped the same way. Set From and To to check only a part.

Does it need my Twitch login?

No. You add the channel by name or link; nothing is connected to your Twitch account.

Is ClipsJudge part of Twitch?

No. ClipsJudge is an independent tool and isn't affiliated with Twitch.
