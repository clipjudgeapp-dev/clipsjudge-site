Questions

When does ClipsJudge launch?

We're clipping real streams right now. Waitlist members get access first, in small groups, before the public launch.

Which platforms does it work with?

Twitch, Kick and YouTube live streams. Clips are vertical with subtitles, made for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels.

Do I need a powerful computer?

No. ClipsJudge runs in the cloud. Your computer can be off; you open your dashboard on any phone or laptop and download the finished clips.

Does it only clip loud moments?

No. It combines chat, voice, movement and what was said, then an AI judge watches the clip before you see it. Funny, awkward and wholesome moments count too.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new account gets 2 hours of clipping over 3 days, with a small badge on the clips. No card needed.

Do plans renew automatically?

No. Each plan is a 30-day pass: you pay once and renew only if you want to. Try everything in the free trial first, because payments aren't refundable except for billing mistakes or a fault on our side. See the refund policy.

Do I need the right to clip a stream?

Yes. Clip your own streams, or streams whose creators allow clipping (many run official clipping programs). Always credit the creator. See our terms.
