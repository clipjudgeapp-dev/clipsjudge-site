Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Try ClipsJudge free first. Because the free trial lets you test everything before you pay, payments aren't refundable, except in the cases below.

Free trial

New accounts get a free trial: 2 hours of video or 3 days, whichever comes first, with watermarked clips and no card needed. Use it to check the clips, the quality and how ClipsJudge works for your streamers before paying.

Plans are 30-day passes

  • Each plan is a 30-day pass paid in advance. Nothing renews automatically and nothing is charged again without you choosing to pay.
  • There's nothing to cancel: if you don't want to continue, simply don't renew. You keep access until your 30 days end.
  • Renewing before your pass ends adds 30 days on top of the time you have left.

Refunds

  • No refunds for change of mind: payments for passes and hour packs aren't refunded once made, including for unused hours or days.
  • Billing mistakes: duplicate or wrong charges are always refunded in full.
  • Service problems: if a fault on our side stops ClipsJudge from working for a significant part of your pass, contact us and we'll refund or credit the affected part.
  • Where the law of your country gives you a right to a refund, we follow it.
  • Accounts closed for breaking our terms aren't refunded.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email clipjudge.app@gmail.com from your account email, with the subject "Refund".
  2. Tell us the date of the payment and what went wrong.
  3. We reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Delivery

ClipsJudge is an online software service. Access starts immediately after sign-up or payment, and clips are delivered in your account for download. Nothing physical is shipped, so there are no shipping charges or delivery times.
